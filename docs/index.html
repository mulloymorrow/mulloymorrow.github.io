I'm Mulloy. I live in Miami, play padel three mornings a week, and spend the rest building Nalya. For 15 years I built recommendation systems at Spotify, Compass, and OfferUp — connecting hundreds of millions of people to music, homes, and marketplaces. The algorithms got very good at finding the thing. They never got good at finding the people to do the thing with. A playlist is just a file until your people are listening with you. A padel court is just a reservation until four humans show up. That gap — between what you love and who you love doing it with — is where I'm spending the next decade. Nalya is the AI that lives in that gap. It's live in Miami with the padel community I play in, and growing from there.
